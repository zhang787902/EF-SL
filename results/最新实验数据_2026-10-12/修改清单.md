**新：**

### [451] 局限 (3)（替换）
**旧：** `(3) Single model testing. The testing is performed with a single model GPT-4o. Generalization to other large language models (Claude, DeepSeek, etc.) is not verified.`

**新：**
> (3) EF-SL safety is not model-agnostic (core limitation). Cross-model experiments (§5.9) show that EF-SL's "safe, no harm" property is not model-independent: it is non-significant on GPT-4o (+0.29%, p=0.711), but exhibits a near-significant negative drift on DeepSeek-V4-Pro (−3.50%, p=0.054) and GLM-5.3 (−3.75%, p=0.067). In other words, EF-SL's safety depends on a strong instruction-following base model; on reasoning models the execution-feedback-triggered rewriting can backfire, degrading EF-SL toward pre-filtering-like harm. We therefore scope EF-SL's claim as "safe on strong instruction-following base models such as GPT-4o," not unconditional safety.

## 十四、新增 §5.9 跨模型验证（插在 §5.8 之后、§6 之前）

在「5.8 Global Correctness Distribution」之后、「6 Discussion」之前，插入如下新小节（含 Table 10，原 §6.1 的 Table 10 相应顺延为 Table 11）：

### 5.9 Cross-Model Validation

The above conclusions rest on a single GPT-4o backbone. To test whether the three claims---pre-filtering harm, EF-SL safety, and the Oracle upper bound---are model-agnostic, we re-ran the five-method comparison under the same protocol with two reasoning-model families, DeepSeek-V4-Pro and GLM-5.3, on the same first 400 Spider Dev samples (aligned with GPT-4o).

**Table 10: Cross-model validation (first 400 Spider Dev samples). Δ = execution-accuracy change (pp) vs. each model's own Baseline; paired McNemar p in parentheses.**

| Method | GPT-4o | DeepSeek-V4-Pro | GLM-5.3 |
|--------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Baseline | 68.50% | 69.50% | 77.25% |
| Lexical SL | −5.50 (p=0.002) | −10.50 (p=1.7×10⁻⁷) | −11.00 (p=2.0×10⁻⁸) |
| LLM-SL | −6.25 (p=1.1×10⁻⁵) | −3.25 (p=0.019) | −0.75 (p=0.728) |
| EF-SL | −0.75 (p=0.581) | −3.50 (p=0.054) | −3.75 (p=0.067) |
| Oracle SL | +6.00 (p=3.9×10⁻⁵) | +4.75 (p=0.004) | +3.75 (p=0.020) |

The three claims diverge sharply in cross-model robustness. **(1) Pre-filtering harm is model-agnostic and stronger on reasoning models**: Lexical SL remains significantly harmful on all three models (−5.50 / −10.50 / −11.00, p ≤ 0.002), with roughly twice the magnitude on DeepSeek and GLM. **(2) The Oracle gain is model-agnostic**: all three models show significant positive gains (+6.00 / +4.75 / +3.75). **(3) EF-SL safety is not model-agnostic (key finding)**: EF-SL is safe on GPT-4o (−0.75%, p=0.581) but shows a clear negative drift on DeepSeek (−3.50%, p=0.054) and GLM (−3.75%, p=0.067) that, while not crossing the p<0.05 threshold, matches the magnitude of LLM-SL harm. EF-SL's safety thus depends on the base model's generation capability; on reasoning models the execution-feedback-triggered rewriting appears to introduce new errors. Accordingly, we scope EF-SL as "a Generate-then-Adjust paradigm safe on strong instruction-following base models (GPT-4o)," not as unconditionally safe.
